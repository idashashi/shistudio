Why does revenue grow while profit stays flat?

Most often there are three reasons. First: customer acquisition cost grows faster than the average order, so each new unit of revenue brings less margin. Second: the range includes negative-margin items that drag the rest down. Third: prices were not revised as costs went up. All three show up in unit economics if you calculate it by segment rather than as a company average.

How can you tell a business needs strategy rather than advertising?

Three signs. First: a bigger ad budget grows revenue but not profit. Second: customers buy once and don’t come back — the problem is the product or price, not traffic. Third: different channels convert equally poorly, which points to the offer, not the channel. If at least two signs match, it is too early to put money into advertising.
