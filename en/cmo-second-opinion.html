When you need it

  • You are hiring a head of marketing and want a candidate’s plan or test assignment reviewed by someone who has been a marketing director themselves.
  • The team is proposing a decision — a new channel, a rebrand, a change of positioning — and you need an outside perspective.

What you get

  • What in the document is right and can be relied on

FAQ

What is a second opinion from a marketing director?

It is a review of one document or decision before you pay for it or adopt it: a marketing strategy, a media plan, a contractor’s commercial proposal, a plan from a candidate for head of marketing. The format is a Telegram chat with short messages, with a response within 3 business days. The cost is €160.

How is a second opinion different from a consultation?

A second opinion is a review of one finished document via Telegram chat, €160, with a response within 3 business days. A consultation is a 90-minute conversation about your business as a whole, €270.

What can I send for review?

A marketing or communication strategy, a media plan with a budget, a contractor’s commercial proposal for advertising, SMM or promotion, a plan or test assignment from a candidate for head of marketing. One document or one decision — one review.

What if there are several documents?

Each document is a separate review. If there are many documents or the decision requires a conversation about the business’s numbers, it is better to go straight for a consultation.

Do you rewrite the document?

No. A second opinion answers the question of whether you can rely on this document and what to fix in it. Rewriting a strategy is a separate piece of work.
